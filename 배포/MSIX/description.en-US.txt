A Windows tray app that controls Spotify with hotkeys, whatever window you're working in. Requires the Spotify desktop app.

• Play/pause, next and previous track with hotkeys
• Change Spotify's volume without touching other apps
• Track toasts with title, artist and album art in the screen corner you choose
• Copy the current track to the clipboard
• Change hotkeys, toast position and language (English/Korean) in Settings

No sign-in or API key needed. Free and open source. Independent project, not affiliated with or endorsed by Spotify AB.
